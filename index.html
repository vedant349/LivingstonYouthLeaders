Who we are

A nonprofit run entirely by students

Livingston Youth Leaders started in May 2021, when a group of Livingston students heard about India's oxygen shortage during the peak of COVID-19 and decided a bake sale was better than doing nothing. It raised and donated $8,150.

Since then we have grown into a year-round group organizing fundraisers, supply drives, and free health clinics. Every project is planned, staffed, and run by middle and high schoolers. The goal is simple. We promote philanthropy among young people and build the next generation of community leaders.

Why join us

Make a real difference

Our track record

Five years of showing up

Most recent first, from a full calendar of drives and clinics back to the bake sale that started it.

2026
  • Winter Coat Drive. Collected new and gently used coats, hats, gloves, and scarves for patients at Trinitas Hospital.
  • Seniors Tech Camp. Set up at Kearny Hindu Temple on 19 April to help 22 senior citizens with everyday tech, from video calls and scam spotting to photos and battery life, with free vitals checks alongside.
  • Essentials Drive. Ran 15 July to 15 August with six drop off points across Livingston, collecting travel size and sample toiletries, then packed them into 110 kits for Franciscan Charities of Newark.
2025
  • Health Screening Camp. Organized with Let's Save Ma, GKHET, and Lions International, offering free health checks. Completed 100 bloodwork panels, 100 vision tests, 100 hearing tests, and 25 mammograms.
2024
  • Fall Fiesta. Held 8 September outside Livingston High School with food stalls, a silent auction, a car wash and a book drive. Raised and donated $5,872 for Bridges Outreach for the Homeless and the Livingston Education Foundation.
  • Medical Equipment and Supplies Drive. An ongoing drive that has so far rehomed 18 pieces of equipment, including crutches, bath benches, walkers, rollators and canes, for uninsured patients in need.
2023
  • Spring Bazaar. A Mother's Day bazaar at the Livingston Oval on 7 May. Raised and donated $8,060 for free breast cancer screenings through Let's Save Ma and for Turkey earthquake relief.
  • Thanksgiving Food Drive. Run with GKHET through to 15 November. Collected 150 cake mixes and 108 stuffing mixes for St James Roman Catholic Church in Newark.
2022
  • Food and Art Fair. Held 5 September at the Livingston Oval with GKHET. Raised and donated $7,980 to fund HPV vaccination for children in India and humanitarian relief through Hope for Ukraine.
2021
  • Bake Sale. Held 23 May at the Livingston Oval. Raised and donated $8,150 for COVID-19 relief in India, funding oxygen concentrators and BiPAP machines in partnership with GKHET.
  • School Supply Drive. Collected backpacks, books and stationery through August for children in Ethiopia with the Future Generations Foundation, with a portion of the books going to schools in Newark.

Organizations we work alongside

Our projects reach further because of the groups who partner with us.

GKHET

Our first partner. Together we sent oxygen concentrators and BiPAP machines to India during the 2021 COVID-19 crisis, and we have collaborated on health projects since.

Let's Save Ma

Free breast cancer screenings and mammography access for women who would otherwise go without.

Lions International

Co-organized our 2025 health screening camp, providing vision and hearing testing at no cost.

Bridges Outreach

Support for people experiencing homelessness across New Jersey and New York.

Franciscan Charities of Newark

Distributes the hygiene kits and essentials we assemble to individuals and families in need.

Livingston Education Foundation

Funds programs and resources for students in our own school district.

Hope for Ukraine

Delivered the humanitarian aid funded by our 2022 Food and Art Fair to families displaced by the war.

Future Generations Foundation

Carried the backpacks and school supplies from our 2021 drive to children in Ethiopia.
