Our story

It started with a bake sale.

In May 2021, a group of Livingston students decided that being young was not a reason to wait.

How we began

May 2021

COVID-19 was at its peak, and news was coming out of India about a massive shortage of oxygen and medical equipment. A group of Livingston students heard about it and decided to do the one thing they knew how to do, which was bake, sell, and raise money. On Sunday May 23, 2021, they set up at the Livingston Oval in front of the library.

They called themselves the Livingston Youth Leaders. That first bake sale raised and donated $8,150. Working with Dr. Sandhya Shah, president of GKHET, the group turned that money into oxygen concentrators and BiPAP machines shipped to India.

It worked. And it proved something the group has run on ever since, that a handful of students with a folding table and a plan can move real resources to people who need them.

Where we are now

Five years later

What began as a one-time response to a crisis became a habit. The annual bake sale turned into a community tradition. Then came art fairs, bazaars, car washes, and drives for school supplies, coats, medical equipment, and hygiene kits.

In 2025 we ran our first full health screening camp, free bloodwork, vision tests, hearing tests, and mammograms with no insurance or age requirements. In 2026 we started teaching senior citizens how to spot phone scams.

Along the way our students have presented the group's work to the Livingston Township Council, standing up in front of local government to explain what they built and why.

Across five years, LYL has raised and donated more than $30,000, and the group is still entirely run by middle and high school students.
