2024 Fall Fiesta
Presented by Livingston Youth Leaders and GKHET
Sunday 8 September 2024, 11am to 4pm
Outside Livingston High School
Proceeds donated to Bridges Outreach for the Homeless and the
  Livingston Education Foundation
Raised and donated $5,872

The day combined several fundraisers in one, food stalls, a silent auction,
a car wash, seeds and saplings with pre-orders, a book drive, clothing and
jewellery, and gift items.

Sponsors and supporters, Sweet Kneads by Ridhima, Starbucks, Eats by Lesley,
Duck Donuts, The Millionaires Closet, Essex Renal Medical Group LLC,
Rubina Sheth Youth and Family Well Being Coach, bee bee designs, Desi Drape
