2023 Thanksgiving Food Drive
Run with GKHET Inc, collection ended 15 November
Collected cake mix in any flavour and dry stuffing mix
Final count, 150 cake mixes and 108 stuffing mixes
Everything collected was donated to St James Roman Catholic Church, Newark NJ
Six drop off points at volunteer homes across Livingston
