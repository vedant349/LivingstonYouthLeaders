2026 Essentials Drive
Small items, big impact
Collection 15 July to 15 August 2026
Six drop off points at volunteer homes across Livingston
Collected travel size and sample toiletries, sorted them into bins, packed them
  into individual paper bags and delivered them to Franciscan Charities of the
  Archdiocese of Newark
110 kits delivered in total
