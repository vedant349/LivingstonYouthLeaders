2023 Spring Bazaar
Mother's Day theme, celebrate Mom and help another Mom
Sunday May 7, 3pm to 6pm
Livingston Oval, Livingston NJ
Funds raised supported breast cancer awareness and free mammogram services
  through Let's Save Ma, and Turkey earthquake relief
Stalls included specialty seeds and saplings, baked goods, Mother's Day themed
  arts and crafts, books, gifts and a raffle for moms, plus samosa, dhokla,
  lemonade and mango lassi
