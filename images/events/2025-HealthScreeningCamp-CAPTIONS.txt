2025 Health Screening Camp
Savour and Screen, A Wellness Event
Friday June 27 2025, 9am to 3pm
Livingston Senior Community Center, 204 Hillside Avenue, Livingston NJ
Co-hosted by Livingston Youth Leaders, GKHET, Let's Save Ma and the
  Livingston Lions Club
Mammograms provided on site by the Mammography in Motion mobile unit,
  University Hospital Newark, with the Connie Dwyer Breast Cancer Foundation
Completed 100 bloodwork panels, 100 vision tests, 100 hearing tests
  and 25 mammograms, 325 screenings in total
