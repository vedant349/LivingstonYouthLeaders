2022 Food and Art Fair
Presented by Livingston Youth Leaders and GKHET
Monday September 5, 2022, 3pm to 7pm
Livingston Oval, Livingston NJ
Funds donated to Hope for Ukraine, and to HPV vaccination for underprivileged
  children in India through GKHET
Menu, samosas, khaman, cookies, brownies, cake pops, mango lassi, lemonade,
  chai and more, alongside a table of student paintings
The research board notes that vaccinating one girl normally costs about $70,
  and about $10 after volume discount
