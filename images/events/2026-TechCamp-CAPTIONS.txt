2026 Tech Camp for Seniors
Sunday 19 April 2026
Kearny Hindu Temple
Helped 22 senior citizens one on one with everyday technology, including video
  calls, spotting scams, finding contacts, organising photos, clearing storage,
  resetting passwords, setting up emergency contacts and battery life
A separate desk offered free blood pressure and vitals checks

NOTE, no original flyer exists for this event. 2026-TechCamp-Flyer.jpg was
created afterwards in the site brand style, so it is a reconstruction rather
than a historical document.
