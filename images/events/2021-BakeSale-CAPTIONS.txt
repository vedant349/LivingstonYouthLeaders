2021 Bake Sale Fundraiser
Sunday May 23 2021, 3pm to 6pm
The Livingston Oval, in front of the library, Livingston NJ
Menu, brownies, choc chip cookies, lemonade, samosas, dhokla, Indian chai,
  plus mango lassi, tie-dye masks, jewellery and student art
100% of proceeds to COVID-19 relief in India, via GKHET Inc and the
  Nesco Jumbo Covid Centre in Mumbai
