2026: Drive

Winter Coat Drive

Over two weeks in January and February we collected new and gently used coats, hats, gloves, and scarves from homes across Livingston, then delivered them to patients at Trinitas Hospital ahead of the coldest weeks of the year.

Trinitas Hospital
2026: Camp

Seniors Tech Camp

A one day help desk at Kearny Hindu Temple where students sat down one on one with 22 senior citizens. Resetting passwords, clearing storage, organising photos, setting up emergency contacts, spotting scams, and making video calls actually work. A second desk offered free blood pressure and vitals checks.

22 seniors helped
2026: Drive

Essentials Drive

Small items, big impact. Through July and August we asked people to hand over the unused travel size bottles from hotel stays, toothpaste and toothbrushes, mouthwash, shampoo, conditioner, and soap. Six drop off points across Livingston, all of it packed into small bags and delivered to Franciscan Charities in Newark.

110 kits delivered
2025: Camp

Health Screening Camp

Our largest project to date, held on 27 June at the Livingston Senior Community Center with Let's Save Ma, GKHET, and the Livingston Lions Club. Mammograms were provided on site by the Mammography in Motion mobile unit from University Hospital Newark. Free to everyone, no insurance needed.

325 screenings in one day
2024: Fundraiser

Fall Fiesta

Our biggest single day, held outside Livingston High School on 8 September. Food stalls, a silent auction, a car wash, seeds and saplings with pre-orders, a book drive, clothing and jewellery. Nine local businesses sponsored it. Proceeds were split between Bridges Outreach for the Homeless and the Livingston Education Foundation.

$5,872 raised and donated
2024: Drive

Medical Equipment and Supplies Drive

An ongoing drive rather than a single day. We collect new and used wheelchairs, walkers, canes, shower benches, adult diapers, and crutches, then match them to uninsured patients in need. So far that is 5 crutches, 6 bath benches and stools, 2 walkers, 2 rollators and 3 canes. If you have any of these at home, we would like to hear from you.

18 pieces of equipment rehomed
2023: Fundraiser

Spring Bazaar

A Mother's Day themed bazaar at the Livingston Oval on 7 May, built around a simple idea. Celebrate Mom and help another Mom. Stalls sold seeds and saplings, baked goods, crafts, books, and gifts. Proceeds funded free breast cancer screenings through Let's Save Ma, plus relief for the Turkey earthquake.

$8,060 raised and donated
2023: Drive

Thanksgiving Food Drive

Run with GKHET, with six drop off points at volunteer homes across Livingston through to 15 November. We asked for two things only, cake mix and dry stuffing mix, and everything collected went to St James Roman Catholic Church in Newark for their Thanksgiving distribution.

150 cake mixes and 108 stuffing mixes
2022: Fundraiser

Food and Art Fair

Student-made food and student-made art, sold at the Livingston Oval on 5 September with GKHET. The students researched the cause themselves and worked out that vaccinating one girl against HPV costs about $70, or around $10 at volume. Proceeds funded HPV vaccination for children in India and humanitarian relief through Hope for Ukraine.

$7,980 raised and donated
2021: Fundraiser

Bake Sale 2021

The project that started Livingston Youth Leaders, held on Sunday 23 May 2021 at the Livingston Oval in response to India's oxygen shortage during the peak of COVID-19. Working with Dr. Sandhya Shah of GKHET, the funds were converted into oxygen concentrators and BiPAP machines shipped directly to India.

$8,150 raised and donated
2021: Drive

School Supply Drive

Collection boxes went up outside homes across Livingston through August, gathering backpacks, books, stationery, lunchboxes, and textbooks. The bulk went to children in Ethiopia through the Future Generations Foundation, and a portion of the books were distributed to schools in Newark.

Future Generations Foundation
